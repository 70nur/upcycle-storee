Brand philosophy

Every year tons of clothes end up in landfills. We give them a second life: each piece is made by hand from old garments, so no two are the same.

Upcycling process

We collect old clothes, sort them by material, wash them, cut them into panels and sew them into new designs. Nothing is dyed with new chemicals.
